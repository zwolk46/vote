### What round 2 shows, and what was changed

All six readers now say what the figure is (the least the result must be worth), what the $8.87 is made of, that the figure is the cost times the odds (the drawn arrow), and that the figures differ because the odds differ; both readers of the visuals alone got these from the marks and labels. What they still miss, and what was done:
- "Why multiply by the odds" (both laypeople and the B reader of the visuals alone): this is the weighing itself, a small sure cost against a small chance of a large result. A first screen cannot teach it without the prose the 04:32 rule cuts; it is the explainer's first job (brief X2, the layperson's sixth question). Left to the explainer, to which the first screen will link.
