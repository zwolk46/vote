`checks/m2/c38_input_volume.py` (2026-11-07, seventh pass) compares, for every state-year with p (208,518 p), n or the contest's total against a count from another file: 38.2 MEDSL precinct files (120, President/US Senate/US House/Governor) vs D1/D2; 38.3 Klarner vs MEDSL legislatures by district (D+R lines where both carry >=80%, else every vote; `c38-districts.json` 249 districts off >5%); 38.4 NCSBE files vs D1/D2/Klarner; 38.5 Indiana in_enr; 38.6 Louisiana; 38.7 D1/D2 vs MEDSL state-level files (House file behind Dataverse guestbook, not taken); 38.8 no-second-count groups + a coarse tier for Klarner chamber-years without MEDSL (votes x N_full/(ref x sum k^2) vs same-structure peers; 34 flagged, mostly multi-member structure; SC Senate 1976/80 3.5x, ID 1984-90 ~2.2x are the state-count candidates); 38.9 one line per state-year -> `c38-state-years.json` (126). `c39_volume_compare.py` reads D3's check-volume.json: both 7, c38 alone 119, D3 alone 3; seven method differences (unit, 2024 gate, fusion, two-party totals, Klarner districts, no second count, subtotal rows).

**Subtotal rule** (m2medsl._subtotal_rows): a row named TOTAL/-1/ALL equal to its county's other rows, or a row whose name the county's other rows extend (parent = longest other name it extends; parents are nobody's child) and equals them, within 0.5%/10 votes (slack only when b>=50). NJ 2024 Bergen's 140 municipality rows (NJ-05 +79% raw, +0.02% after); ID 2022 COUNTY TOTAL rows; NJ 2018 Camden's "... DIST n TOTAL" precincts are real rows.

**Findings and the factor on p:** ID 2022 SD-1 (Klarner unopposed, MEDSL contested 59/41; p 0 -> positive; #97 took it to not computed, write-in rule) and VT 2018 Chittenden (Klarner 3.7x MEDSL; #97 moved it x3.49) sent at once; NH 2022 Senate exactly 2x in 24/24, House +54.9% (#95 moved p to MEDSL's counts); NY 2024 Monroe lacks US House rows in 200/659 precincts and Assembly rows in 185 (29% of its presidential vote), Ontario Assembly rows 54/92 -> AD 135-138 p rest on short n, too high by up to ~x1.4, plus 8 Monroe local p; Klarner districts off 2x vs MEDSL (MI 2022 lower 63 x1.9, upper 3 x2.35; NY 2022 upper 56 +54%; NM 2022 lower 53 +78%; IL 2022 lower 2 -70%; FL 2018 lower 40 -74%): p rests on Klarner, neither right by default; D2's governor total_votes = two-party where the source gives no more (RI 2018 +11%, WV 2024 +7%, WY 2022 +6%, IN 2024 +5%, MO/NH 2024, NH 2018, NC 2020): no p (n is two-party by design); ME-02 2024 record adds the Clerk's RCV lines ("Continuing Ballots" 391,596) into total 794,532 and pairs them in two_party; p not computed, label only; NC 2002 House 118: Klarner's CONTEST file says 125,193 but the record (candidate file) and the Board agree at 25,718, no p; WA 2022 WA-08 file -8.3% (lean of 2024 King/Pierce local p shifts a little); ND 2024 governor rows absent in Burleigh/Divide (no p rests there).
