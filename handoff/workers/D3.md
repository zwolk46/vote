**Done (committed on the branch, not yet merged):**
- `local_districts.coverage(year)` (`COVER_VERSION` 12): county by county, for each family of contests on every ballot below the state's (the lower chamber, the upper chamber, the US House by district), the precincts of the county's top contest its rows miss. The top contest is the candidate contest with votes in the most precincts (a question only where there is none), each precinct's votes its TOTAL mode's or its other modes' sum, whichever is more (Delaware 2018 gives some candidates a TOTAL line of 0). Precincts missed are the set difference but no more than the difference in counts (Virginia 2018 names a question's precincts apart). Precinct names are folded for case and spacing, and a name the top contest gives once is matched under another jurisdiction code or as a '/'-joined part (Maine 2024). A special contest's rows count for its family, though never as the top contest (Texas 2024 files the regular TX-18 contest special). A US House row with no district counts (Santa Cruz County, Arizona, 2022); a chamber's row with no district does not (22:42 item 4, Maricopa 2016). Lines that are no contest (straight party, registered voters, ballots cast, turnout, voting statistics) are left out. Each district's counties come from MEDSL's files on the same lines (2016+2018, 2022+2024). Cached in `$VOTE_CACHE/volume/normalized/coverage-<year>.json` (about 4.5 minutes a year, the four in parallel). A staggered upper chamber is read by the lower chamber's rows or the top contest (22:42 item 3); a chamber is all up where its districts are 90% of its most on the same lines (Vermont's 2022 chambers have more districts).
- `check()` writes `incomplete_counties` (the county gate) and `statewide_short` into `data/local/check-districts.json`:
  - The county gate is read from the US House's rows only, since Florida, Oklahoma, Texas and Kansas 2022 leave unopposed legislative seats off the ballot or the file. A county is short where those rows miss more than 2% of its precincts holding more than 2% of the top contest's votes. A state that left a US House seat off the ballot (D2's record: no votes) is not read. Each short county lists `whole_contests`, the contests whose own rows are in its precincts (within 2%): their district is the county, so they are not held by it; a legislative seat is held only where its own chamber's rows are short in the county (22:26 (a)) [my readings]. v12 hits: see REPORT §32.
  - `statewide_short`: a statewide contest (rows in 90% of the state's counties) whose rows miss more than 2% of a county's precincts. Held where the votes missed are more than 2% of the state's (its district is the state), else noted [my reading]. A contest in 90% of a state's counties is gated as statewide (New York 2024's Proposal One, held).

## How to rebuild
1. `VOTE_CACHE=/tmp/vote-cache python3 -I pipeline/local_volume.py check`. The build reads its output.
2. Commit the code.
3. Run `bash /tmp/vote-cache/rebuild.sh`, about 3.5 minutes. It runs `intake/va_elect.py`, `local.py build --with-p`, `local_medsl.py build`, and `local_ncsbe.py check` and `build`.
4. Run `local_la.py`, `local_nj.py`, `local_nj_leg.py`, `local_va_leg.py` and `local_va.py check` (the last writes `data/local/check-va-local.json`, Virginia's 2023 local gate).
5. Revert the files whose only change is a stamp (`commit`, `read_utc`, `computed_by`).
6. List the moves against the base, record by record (p moved, lost or gained, notes, reasons).

Never use `git commit -a`, `git stash` or `git checkout -- data/`.

## Rules that bind (from the orchestrator and the law)
- A refusal binds the whole agency: stop and report.
- A count of record needs a printed official or certified mark, or the agency's own words on the linking page. A file name is not a mark (21:55). A third party's copy never counts (21:03).
- One request every few seconds per host, with User-Agent `vote-data-research/1.0`. Never put the maker's email anywhere.
- Messages to the orchestrator are 2,000 characters at most.
