## The data and the rules it follows

`src/data/build-field.mjs` reads `data/` and writes `src/data/field.json` (run from the repository root:
`node src/data/build-field.mjs`). The rules, each the orchestrator's or the maker's, are in its header comment:
- every contest with a p at the levels M2 has checked (president, Senate, governor, House, state legislatures); local
  races and measures wait for M2. One dot = 10 contests.
- named figures only from p that are the contest's own: state legislatures only `forecast_scope` "district (class A/B)";
  class C and like-race routes are drawn as lighter dots, never named (09:31; law 8).
- the lead is the state with the **highest chance** in 2024 (Nevada), never called "the closest state"; West Virginia
  is "the lowest chance of any state" (11:05). The explainer names Gelman, Silver and Edlin's 2008 states the same way.
- each level's named low is **2024's** (11:05).
- a lean note ("so p may be low") is a dashed arrow, not prose; governors' and Senate notes are ruled out (`LEAN_OUT`)
  until D2 removes them (11:05, 11:16). Only the House arrow is drawn.
- populations: president = nation, Senate/governor = state, House = district, state legislatures from
  `data/population/state-legislative-{2022,2024}.json` (#71). Where none exists the dot or figure fades **without
  words** (12:01; Zach 04:32).
- every sentence on the page is justified in `design/TEXT-LEDGER.md` (Zach 04:32: visuals over prose).
