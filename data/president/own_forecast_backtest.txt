Back-test of the model's own forecast: every year forecast from the years before it only.
Brier: the Brier score of the 51 state win probabilities (lower is better; 0.25 is a coin toss).
in50, in90: the share of states whose result fell inside the forecast's central 50% and 90%
intervals (a calibrated forecast: 0.50 and 0.90). MAE: mean absolute error of the state means.
P(D): the forecast's chance that the Democrat wins the College (a 269 tie counted half); won: 1 if
the Democrat won. 538 columns: 538's published forecast, the last dated before Election Day
(polls-plus where it ran several; 2008's file has Election Day only). polls: two_months_out uses
538's national average of 9 September; eve its last average before Election Day (2024: 12 September,
the last in the files, so 2024 is not in the eve runs). The shrunk variant's 1984 forecast rests on a
shrink factor fitted to two cycles (1976, 1980), which is why its 1984 row is degenerate.
