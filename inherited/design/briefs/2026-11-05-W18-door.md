# W18: the sign-in page (the door), large, loud and deliberate

Read first, whole: `LAW.md`, `the-law-for-agents.md`, `RULINGS.md` (from 2026-11-05 16:44 on; in particular 17:47 and 18:25), `STATE.md`, `briefs/_COMMON.md`, research/plan/2026-11-05-A-maker-principles.md.

**Goal.** Redo the door, the sign-in page at midterms.zwolk.com that strangers see first, on phones and on desktop, so that it is in the maker's words "really deliberate, large, loud, exciting". *Reason:* the maker, 18:25 UTC 11-05, verbatim in RULINGS.md. He wants as much effort as possible and accepts that it takes a while.

**What is there.** The door is lib/gate/page.js, which middleware.js writes for each request. It has:
- a semicircle of 435 grey seats with two sweeping bands;
- the flag logo tile in its hollow, a wordmark, a lede, and the passcode form (or the invite form);
- one script: the glass reveal in lib/gate/glass.js (#153), ported from 21st.dev's liquid-glass-logo-reveal (shader from zanwei/glass-icon-reveal, MIT). It runs under a per-request nonce in the content security policy.

On the way in, a view transition carries the semicircle and wordmark to the home page's arrival cover (index.html: ar-arc, ar-word).

**His asks, each to be met:**
1. "a little bit of snapping at the end of the animation". The likely cause is the hand-over from the canvas's last frame to the SVG tile (glass.js `giveBack`): a size, position or rendering difference. Find the real cause and make the end seamless.
2. "the other things in the background should [not] appear until the glass animation is done". The glass plays first, alone. Everything else waits for it.
3. "it should be larger". The glass reveal and the logo become the page's hero, large on both phone and desktop.
4. "each element should deliberately appear". After the glass, the seats, wordmark, lede, form and note come in one by one, each with its own entrance, in an order that reads as a sequence.
5. "more noticeable motion, maybe like red and blue flickers for the dots, or like a waving flag animation". Make it noticeable. **Limit (law 7, and the door's rule that no seat is shown as won):** no seat may settle in a party's colour or read as a count. A flag wave of red and white stripes rippling across the seats, or flickers that never settle and are plainly decoration, are both fine. The choice is yours; say why you made it.

**Keep:**
- The form works without the script.
- The script only by nonce. Keep `tests/gate.test.js` passing, and extend it if you add scripts.
- Reduced motion gives a calm, complete page.
- With no WebGL2 the page is still whole.
- The view transition to the home page.
- 44 px targets.
- No soft shadows or hover glows (principles 2b), apart from what he asked for.

*Reason:* these are law, earlier rulings, or the arrival that is already live.
